# Motion

## Feel

Expensive motion is smooth, overlapped, and rhythmically uneven. Cheap motion is linear, snappy-for-no-reason, and always the same speed.

## Techniques

1. Smoothness
   - Ease the keyframe graphs. Overlap incoming and outgoing so one move has not finished when the next starts
   - Default curve — cubic-bezier(0.4, 0.0, 0.2, 1) or a soft S-curve with tiny overshoot
   - Hero moves live in 0.4–0.8s. Micro-UI in 0.2–0.4s
   - Never linear on position/scale/rotation of the product. Linear reads cheap

2. Dynamic transitions
   - When one scene flows into the next, the viewer should think it belongs there
   - Prefer a shared object, shared axis, or shared color across the cut
   - Hard cuts only when the message needs them
   - Cross-dissolve without a spatial idea is also cheap — always have a graphic reason

3. Adaptive rhythm
   - Do not keep one tempo the whole video
   - Hook can be slower (regal). Proof can lift. Resolve breathes again
   - One element moves at a time unless a designed stagger is the idea
   - Put holds between moves. The hold is part of the luxury

## Composition while moving

- Keep the hero readable at mid-move — no motion blur soup
- Parallax is a whisper (2–6% scale / few px) not a ride
- Z-depth + slight scale beats XY sliding everything

## Ban list

- Bounce presets on premium products
- Elastic overshoot that feels cartoony
- Spinning logos
- Particle glitter, light leaks, film grain as a crutch
- Four things entering at once
