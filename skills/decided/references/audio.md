# Audio

Audio sets energy. Treat it as brand, not decoration.

## Music BPM

| BPM | Feeling | Use |
|---|---|---|
| 60–80 | Regal, cinematic, heritage | Flagship, craft, quiet luxury |
| 90–110 | Smooth, cool, effortless | Default premium product |
| 115–123 | Elite, kinetic, sophisticated | Feature density, performance |
| 124+ | Drive / hype | Only if the brief is launch-hype. Easy to cheapen |

Stay inside one band for a series of videos.

## Genre

Match audience. No default rap, trailers-from-a-template, or stock "inspiring corporate" if the brand is quiet-premium.

## Sound design

Same psychology as branding — if it is not important, it does not exist.

- SFX only when they explain a physical or UI event, keep people engaged, or help them understand the product
- After the mix, listen to the whole piece and ask what feels off
- Remove anything too loud, out of place, cute, or not doing one of those jobs
- Whooshes sit under the music, not on top of the dialogue-less mix
- No comic pops, risers-every-cut, rap-as-default, or repeating the same hit

## Mix notes for implementers

- Music bed continuous; duck 2–4dB under a hero SFX
- Silence is allowed and often correct on the last hold
- If shipping Remotion without a licensed track, leave a clearly marked `Audio` slot and specify BPM + mood rather than a random MP3
