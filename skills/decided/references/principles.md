# Source principles (Leo / Intently)

Encode the article. Do not dilute into generic "make it look like Apple."

A subagent without this file will reach for black backgrounds, SF Pro, and springs. That is the costume. These rules are the reason the costume works.

## 1. Branding — premium storytelling

The strongest brands are not selling products. They give people a way to identify through their choices.

Apple videos are not awareness. The world already knows Apple. The job is to make people who already know the brand feel special for using it.

Ideology:
- Intention is everything
- Nothing is done on accident
- Every choice needs a purpose — font, background, music, color theme
- A huge part of premium is refusing things that make the brand feel less premium

Where people fail: too many effects, unnecessary SFX, rap as default.

Give yourself real rules and keep them on the same type of work:
- 3 colors
- same backgrounds
- similar music family for that project type

To the audience it should feel like you decided it had to be that way. Consistency is how "decided" reads.

Identity test: if you mute the logo, would a regular user still feel "this is for people like me"? If the piece only lists features, it failed branding.

## 2. Design first

Every great video starts as still design. Do not hire motion to invent the look.

Apple locks typography, backgrounds, and the rest in a guideline and repeats it video to video. Product designers storyboard frame by frame (Figma-level stills) before animation exists.

Principles:
- One shot = one idea. Do not try to show everything
- Every scene has space to breathe
- Key objects sit in the center of the frame
- Background follows the guideline and never interrupts the key objects
- Quiet field (usually black or white, dead quiet). Color lives on the product or the UI
- Never bright-on-bright. Contrast does the work
- A couple typefaces max, always from the brand system — titles, UI, disclaimers, same rules the whole piece
- People will not name the type. They will just feel it is the brand

Gate: if a mid-hold still would look cheap as a poster, do not animate or ship that scene/section.

## 3. Animation

1. Smoothness — ease graphs, overlap incoming and outgoing. Linear reads out of place and cheap.
2. Dynamic transitions — when one scene flows into the next, the viewer thinks it belongs there. Avoid direct cuts unless the message needs them.
3. Adaptive rhythm — not one speed the whole way. Speed up, slow down, or hold depending on the part.

## 4. Music

The track is how you control energy, including the expensive feeling.

- 60–80 BPM — regal, cinematic, heritage
- 90–110 BPM — smooth, cool, effortless
- 115–123 BPM — elite, kinetic, sophisticated
- above that — drive and hype; can fit or destroy

Pick BPM first. Then genre for audience. Stay in one band across a series.

## 5. Sound design

Same psychology as branding. If it is not important, it does not exist.

After SFX, listen to the whole piece and ask what feels off.
Remove anything too loud, out of place, or that does not help people stay engaged or understand the product.

## Translation map (video → web)

| Video rule | Web equivalent |
|---|---|
| Not awareness, belonging | Homepage promise is identity, not a feature grid |
| 3 colors / same backgrounds / same music family | One token file. No page-level extra accent |
| One shot = one idea | One section = one idea. Split packed blocks |
| Space to breathe | Real section padding. Empty field around the product |
| Object in the center | Hero and product on one axis, not a collage |
| Bg never interrupts | No texture, particle, or autoplay video behind the product |
| Type system on titles + UI + legal | Same family on nav, headlines, UI chrome, footnotes |
| Ease + overlap | No linear hero motion. Overlap enter/exit |
| Scene belongs to the next | Scroll/route transitions share object, color, or axis |
| Adaptive rhythm | Not the same fade-up on every section. Hero slow, proof lifts, close holds |
| Listen-and-cut SFX | Motion/SFX/hover pass: delete anything that does not explain |

## Naive-agent tells (reject these)

- "Apple style" = black + blur + spring + 12 features in the first view
- Adding glow, glass, particles, or a track to hide a weak still
- New type or color "just for this section / this hover"
- Linear or bounce on the product
- Hard cut / instant route change with no graphic reason
- Rap or trailer music on a quiet-premium brief
- Invented metrics, press rows, or UI labels
- Designing motion before locked stills
