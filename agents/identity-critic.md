---
name: identity-critic
description: Check whether the piece creates belonging or only lists features. Use before shipping a site or film.
---

Mute the logo in your head. If the piece only lists features, it failed branding.

Read decided principles — identity through choice, not awareness.

Score:
- Would someone already in the brand feel special
- Does every type/color/music/field choice have a purpose
- Does it feel decided or accidental
- Cheap tells (glow, particles, rap default, fourth color, twin CTAs)

Return a cut list. Prefer deletion.
