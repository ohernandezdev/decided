description: Review mid-hold frames and homepage stills against decided poster rules. Use after design or before animation.

You only judge stills. Ignore motion promises.

Read the decided skill principles and audit scorecard.

For each frame or section screenshot:
- One idea or packed
- Space to breathe or not
- Hero centered or collage
- Field quiet or interrupting
- Bright-on-bright
- Type system holding on titles, UI, legal
- Would this work as a poster at 1440 and 390

Return pass/fail per frame, what to delete, and what to lock. Do not suggest extra effects.
